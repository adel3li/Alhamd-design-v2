# Detailed findings: Landing page and legacy v1

The complete list from the line-by-line read of this area, at commit `68e723a`. The items that matter most are also in [FINDINGS.md](../FINDINGS.md) with ids; this file keeps the long tail. The code is described in [10-landing-page.md](../reference/10-landing-page.md).

Each bullet reads: `[area] [severity] title — location — what is wrong and why — suggested direction`. Unless a bullet says otherwise it was established by reading the code, not by running it.

Checked against commit `68e723a`. "Measured" means verified on 2026-11-17 with a scratch `vite build` (Vite 6.4.4) loaded in headless Chromium, Google Fonts blocked. "Run" means executed with `tsx`/`tsc`/`node` on a read-only basis.

## Landing page

- [content] [high] Landing promises scholar review before publishing, but every shipped text is unreviewed — src/landing/LandingPage.tsx:34 — «وكلّ نصٍّ يمرّ على أهل العلم قبل نشره» contradicts the data: all 40 blessings have `reviewStatus: "pending_review"`, `quran.verified: false`, `hadith.gradeVerified: false` (src/data/blessings_ar.json:3 `contentNote` says so), the gate is off (src/data/loader.ts:4), and the app itself says «قيد المراجعة العلمية» (src/components/screens/ContentReviewModal.tsx:31). A public religious-content claim that is false today — reword to "under review" now, or finish the review and turn the gate on before keeping the sentence.
- [content] [high] "No counter, no streaks" promise is contradicted by the app — src/landing/LandingPage.tsx:29,40 — «بلا عدّاد ولا أيّامٍ «فائتة»» and «لا نقاط ولا شارات ولا سلاسل أيّام», while the consistency screen computes and shows a consecutive-day count «أيام التدبر المتتالية» and charts it (src/components/screens/ConsistencyScreen.tsx:60-79,178) — remove the streak UI from the app or drop the claim.
- [content] [med] "Free" claim sits next to a paywalled feature — src/landing/LandingPage.tsx:27,338 — «مجّانًا» with «رحلتي» listed as a feature; رحلتي is gated after 2 stations (src/lib/entitlements.ts:3) with placeholder prices (src/components/screens/PremiumPaywallScreen.tsx:191,226) — say which parts are free, as the paywall itself does at PremiumPaywallScreen.tsx:293.
- [content] [med] "Verses in Uthmani script" is not true of the data, and the mockup shows text the app does not contain — src/landing/LandingPage.tsx:34, src/landing/PhoneMockups.tsx:131 — the mock verse is typed with Uthmani orthography (`ٱلْيَوْمَ … ٱلْإِسْلَٰمَ دِينًۭا`); the same verse in src/data/blessings_ar.json:14 is in simplified spelling and none of the 40 `textUthmani` values contains U+0671 — replace the data with a verified Uthmani source or change the claim; render the mock from the data.
